Who We Are

Powering The Nation's Journey Since 1998

Railway Services is the nation's most trusted rail network operator, serving millions of passengers and businesses across 7,200+ destinations every day.

From high-speed Rajdhani routes to heritage mountain railways, we blend modern technology with the timeless charm of train travel.
